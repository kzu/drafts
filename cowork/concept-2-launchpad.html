Drop invoices in a folder. Invoice Intake reads each one, matches it to a purchase order, asks you about the odd ones, then files the PDFs and adds a row per invoice to your spreadsheet.

  1. 1Read the files

  2. 2Pull out the details

  3. 3Match to purchase orders

    Looks each one up in your spreadsheet.

  4. 4Review exceptions

    Shows you the ones that did not match.

    Asks you
  5. 5File and log

    Moves the PDFs, adds the rows.
