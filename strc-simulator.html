How it works

The rules

  • Every share is bought at $100, STRC’s stated amount, and valued at $100: market prices, past or future, are ignored. You buy on the chosen day (the next trading day when markets were closed), and the run lasts the chosen number of years from there.
  • Up to the latest data () the run gets every dividend Strategy actually declared, by ex-dividend date. After it, every month pays the dividend of the latest declared month (), daily.
  • A dividend goes to the shares held before its ex-dividend date: shares bought on a day earn from the next ex-dividend date on. STRC settles in one business day, so the ex-dividend date is the record date.
  • Daily dividends, as Strategy declares them: each month pays a twelfth of the annual rate on the $100 stated amount (12% pays $1.00 a share), spread over its calendar days, weekends and holidays included, so the running total is cut to the cent and the last day takes the rest. At 12% in a 30-day month a share gets $0.03 most days and $0.04 every third day. A weekend or holiday day goes ex-dividend with the business day before it, and each day is paid the next business day.
  • Reinvesting buys STRC at $100 on the payment day: fractional shares for the whole amount, or whole shares only, with the rest waiting in cash that earns nothing until it reaches $100. Taking cash keeps the shares as they are; the total adds up the position and the cash taken out. A dividend earned but not paid by the end of the run counts in the total.
  • Past payment days: the next business day after the ex-dividend date, except the monthly dividends through June 2026, paid on the last business day of the month.

Assumptions and limits

  • No taxes, fees or commissions. Strategy expects STRC’s distributions to be a return of capital for years, which lowers your cost basis instead of being taxed as income; that changes with your situation and the company’s earnings, and this page does not model it.
  • STRC trades above and below $100, so buying or selling at market prices gives a different result. Strategy adjusts the rate every month to keep it near $100.
  • The rate is not guaranteed: Strategy’s board sets it every month and may lower it, and it can suspend dividends. STRC is not backed by Strategy’s bitcoin; it has a preferred claim on what is left of the company.
  • Strategy can redeem STRC at $101 plus accrued dividends; a projection running for decades assumes it never does.
  • Weekends are the only days markets are assumed closed after the latest data.
