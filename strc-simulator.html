STRC simulator: Stretch preferred with daily dividends

Put money into Strategy’s Variable Rate Series A Perpetual Stretch Preferred (STRC) on any day since its July 2025 IPO, hold it for up to 30 years, and either reinvest every dividend or take it as cash. The past uses STRC’s real closes and dividends; from the latest close on, dividends accrue every calendar day and are paid the next business day, as they have been since November 2026, at the current month’s dividend and the price you choose.

How it works

The rules

  • You buy at the close of the chosen day (the next trading day when markets were closed), and the run lasts the chosen number of years from there. Up to the latest close () it uses STRC’s real closes and every dividend actually declared, by ex-dividend date. After it, prices are the price you set, and every month pays the dividend of the latest declared month () on the cadence you set.
  • A dividend goes to the shares held before its ex-dividend date: shares bought at a close earn from the next ex-dividend date on. STRC settles in one business day, so the ex-dividend date is the record date.
  • Daily dividends, as Strategy declares them: each month pays a twelfth of the annual rate on the $100 stated amount (12% pays $1.00 a share), spread over its calendar days, weekends and holidays included, so the running total is cut to the cent and the last day takes the rest. At 12% in a 30-day month a share gets $0.03 most days and $0.04 every third day. A weekend or holiday day goes ex-dividend with the business day before it, and each day is paid the next business day.
  • Twice a month pays what accrued on the 15th and the last day of the month, monthly on the last day; each is paid the next business day. A period still open at the end of the run counts what accrued so far.
  • Reinvesting buys STRC at the close of the payment day: fractional shares for the whole amount, or whole shares only, with the rest waiting in cash that earns nothing. Taking cash keeps the shares as they are; the total adds up the position and the cash taken out.
  • Past payment days: next business day after the ex-dividend date for the daily and twice-a-month dividends; the last business day of the month for the monthly dividends through June 2026.
  • The effective yields compound the current rate on the price you set: the yield on price is the rate × $100 ÷ price, and reinvesting it n times a year gives (1 + yield ÷ n)n − 1.

Assumptions and limits

  • No taxes, fees or commissions. Strategy expects STRC’s distributions to be a return of capital for years, which lowers your cost basis instead of being taxed as income; that changes with your situation and the company’s earnings, and this page does not model it.
  • The rate is not guaranteed: Strategy’s board sets it every month and may lower it, and it can suspend dividends. STRC is not backed by Strategy’s bitcoin; it has a preferred claim on what is left of the company. Its lowest close since the IPO was .
  • Strategy can redeem STRC at $101 plus accrued dividends; a projection running for decades assumes it never does.
  • The projection keeps the latest declared dividend and one flat price for every future month, and weekends are the only days markets are assumed closed.
